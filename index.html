Time40 minutes (5 planning + 30 writing + 5 checking)
Lengthabout 250–300 words
GradeNone. This essay is not graded. Your teacher will use it to plan lessons for your group, so write honestly and naturally.
Remember
  • Use the grammar you normally use. Do not try to use structures you are unsure about just to impress your teacher.
  • Use a formal, academic style if you can (for example, avoid a lot of and kids).
  • Do not use a dictionary, a phone or a translator. Do not ask for help.
  • If you do not know a word, use a simpler word and keep writing.

Setting Up the Error Log

A

Study a model entryabout 8 minutes

The error log is one of the most important documents in this course. In it, you record your errors, correct them, and explain why they happened. Students who keep a careful log improve faster.

Read the model entry. Then discuss the questions with a partner.

Part of the entryModel
My sentence (with error)I am living in this city since 2019.
CorrectionI have lived in this city since 2019.
Error typeTense and aspect: present perfect vs. present continuous
Reason (why?)Since 2019 connects a past starting point to now. The present continuous cannot show this, so I need a perfect form.
Tip for next timeSince or for + a period up to now → use a present perfect form.
Discuss
1Compare these two reasons. Which is more useful, and why?
  • Reason 1: “It is wrong.”
  • Reason 2: “Since 2019 connects the past to now, so I need a perfect form.”
2Why is the “Tip for next time” column helpful?
B

Check a sampleabout 12 minutes

Work in pairs. Read the six sentences. One sentence is correct. For each of the other five, write a correction and a one-sentence reason that begins with Because… or This needs… since…. For the correct sentence, write OK.

1The company has launched its new app in 2022, and downloads have risen steadily since then.
2By the time the manager arrived, we already finished the slides.
3I am knowing the author's earlier work quite well.
4She has been working on the report all week, and she still has two sections to write.
5While I was reading the article, I have found a surprising statistic.
6The committee has been approving the budget, so you can now order the equipment.
Useful language
  • The error is in…
  • This sentence needs… because…
  • The time expression shows…
  • This verb describes a state, not an action.
